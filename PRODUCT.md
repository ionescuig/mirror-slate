# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The human walks past a wall at home. The glance lasts a few seconds. The human also opens the glass on a desktop. The wall display is a Galaxy Tab A7 Lite (SM-T220) behind double-sided mirror film. The panel is glossy and dim. The film lowers contrast.

## Product Purpose

The glass shows the time, today's weather, the next five days, one list, and the calendar. The human can read all four regions without a lean-in. The display does not ask for a login.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, literal, and silver. The physical mirror is the material. The glass disappears into that film.

## Anti-references

Do not copy Magic Mirror compliments, news, or extra modules. A monochrome icon may sit beside a weather word, a task, or an event. Do not copy an icon grid. Do not copy a DAKboard photo collage or a DAKboard watermark. Do not use dashboard cards, gradient text, blur, or a blue smart-home theme. Do not use `#fff`. The ground is `#000`.

## Design Principles

1. The film limits the design. Type must stay readable on a 360-nit panel.
2. Size shows importance. Today is larger. Color does not shout.
3. The glass has four regions. Do not add a fifth region.
4. The display does not ask for an account. The host already has consent.
5. A task is a line. An event is a line. A forecast day is a line.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use large type and high contrast for the film. The list control is at least 48px tall. Honor `prefers-reduced-motion`. The clock changes. The glass does not animate. Text carries the fact. An icon does not replace a time, a temperature, or a title.
