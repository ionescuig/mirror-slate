Privacy policy

mirror-slate reads two kinds of Google data, and only for the owner of the Google account:

  • Google Calendar events, read only.
  • Google Tasks, read only.

The computer at home stores the access grant. The display receives the calendar rows and the task rows. mirror-slate does not sell this data. It does not share this data with other people. It does not use this data for ads.

The owner can remove mirror-slate from their Google Account, under Third-party access. After that, the display can no longer read the calendar or the tasks.
